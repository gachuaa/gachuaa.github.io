I'm starting this blog to keep the useful parts of my learning in one place: the steps that worked, the mistakes that taught me something, and the explanations I want to remember.

## What I'll share

- [Machine write-ups](/categories/machine-write-ups/): how I approached a lab and connected the clues.
- [Web security](/categories/web-security/): concepts, testing techniques, and small experiments.
- [Linux](/categories/linux/): commands, setup notes, and problems I solved.
- [Networking](/categories/networking/): protocols and practical troubleshooting.

## What makes a useful note

A command is more useful when I understand why I'm running it. A write-up is more useful when it includes the reasoning between the steps.

That's what I want to capture here. As I learn more, I'll return to these posts and improve them.
