I'm Gachuaa. I'm learning cybersecurity and using this blog to document what I figure out along the way.

## What you'll find here

- **Machine write-ups:** the path through a lab, including the clues, commands, and reasoning behind each step.
- **Web security:** notes on how web applications work and how vulnerabilities happen.
- **Linux:** useful commands, configuration, and fixes worth keeping.
- **Networking:** protocols, troubleshooting, and practical experiments.

## How I want to write

I want each post to explain why something worked, what I tried when it didn't, and what I learned from it. Clear steps, useful screenshots, and enough context to follow the idea.

This is a record of learning, with room to revisit and improve the notes as I understand more.

You can find my projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/gachuaa).
